(`@tabler/icons-webfont` 3.31.0, `tabler-icons.ttf`), embedded in FiresAllTheItems.dll:

| fati_piece.png | home | U+EAC1 |

| fati_armor.png | shield | U+EB24 |

## VNEI

FiresAllTheItems began as a port of **VNEI** (Valheim Not Enough Items) by Maximilian Schmöcker, without its Jotunn
dependency. The item index, recipe lookup, category buckets, favourites file, known-recipe refresh and the default
item blacklist follow VNEI's design and code, rewritten for Valheim 1.0.

Source: https://github.com/MSchmoecker/VNEI
